Questions & answers

Do I need to book?

We recommend booking ahead, especially at weekends. Message us on WhatsApp or call +357 97 889963.

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothing so you can relax and move easily during your massage.

How long is a session?

Sessions range from 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the massage you choose. See the prices above.

Can I come with a partner?

Yes. Choose one of our couples packages above and we will prepare a room for two.

Do you offer massage during pregnancy?

Yes, we offer a Pregnancy Massage. Please tell us about your pregnancy when you book so we can prepare for you.
